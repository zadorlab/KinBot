# PES evidence and duplicate review, 21 October 2026

The current supplied inventory contains 26 accepted CH3S search routes across
8 unordered endpoint pairs, and 42 CH3SS routes across 19 pairs. The counts
include reverse discoveries and different reaction templates. They are not
counts of distinct stationary saddles. The full supplied inventory, node key,
and topology maps are in [the evidence report](pes_evidence_2026-10-21/README.md).

## Changes

- The monitor distinguishes retained child branch reports predating a new PES
  controller even when that child has not yet restarted. Underlying failed QC
  records remain visible; this does not mark them recovered.
- The monitor shows accepted product-group counts separately from search-route
  counts. Refresh reports local aliases, cross-well aliases, graphs to archive,
  and allocation cancellations separately.
- Composite workers publish `accepted_l2_network.json` when exploration
  finishes and after a live revisit. This exposes accepted endpoints, L2
  structures/energies/frequencies and pathway classes before CBH construction.
  A CBH planning failure no longer prevents this diagnostic snapshot.
- New automatic plans carry available, validated stereochemical pathway
  classes before graph reuse. A legacy plan may acquire this missing metadata
  only when all other scientific inputs and membership are unchanged. The old
  plan is archived; native QC graphs are not moved or resubmitted.
- Saved local aliases are rechecked against current endpoint, pathway,
  geometry, energy and frequency criteria. A contradictory alias stops for
  review; it is not silently retained or expanded.
- `python -m kinbot.run_pes_audit` reports the topology, actual mappings,
  unmatched pairs and their numerical differences, missing evidence, soft
  imaginary-mode review flags, and recorded worker failures. It writes JSON,
  a bounded text summary, and DOT topology files outside the run. Optional
  database reads use SQLite `mode=ro`; they never invoke QC or recovery.

## What is still unresolved

The accepted monitor rows alone cannot establish which remaining searches are
the same saddle, which are distinct pathways later removed by the outer PES's
lowest-path selection, or which have questionable IRC/mode evidence. Strict
graph equivalence and kinetic pathway selection are different operations.
The audit-only change did not move pathway selection before ANL. The follow-up
[outer-PES selection change](OUTER_PES_ANL_SELECTION_2026-10-21.md) addresses
that scheduling gap: the PES owner selects L2 routes before dispatching new
ANL work. It does not substitute a different saddle's composite energy merely
because its endpoints match. Strict graph reuse remains a separate decision.

Cache rows in the audit are explicitly unverified evidence, not a salvage
grant. A current manifest/snapshot and corresponding IRC/pathway records are
needed before retiring additional expensive graphs. Input-geometry labels do
not establish final optimized stereochemistry or a literature assignment.

The latest supplied monitor (10:56:52 PDT) still shows exploration running,
the old descriptor failures waiting for downstream recovery, and old SH/VRC
failures awaiting their branches. Three CH3SS stage-four TS rejections lack
full current native/geometry-acceptance reasons. Job 52910850 retains its old
one-day allocation limit; controller defaults cannot retroactively extend it.
The new audit and monitor work immediately after pulling; new snapshot and
planner behavior require a later controlled controller refresh.
